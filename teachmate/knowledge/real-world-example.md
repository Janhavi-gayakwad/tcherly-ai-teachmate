# Anchor an abstract idea in a concrete real-world example
summary: Introduce or close an abstract concept with a familiar, concrete situation where it is used.
helps_with: boring or difficult abstract topics; Boring reasons "Not meaningful content" or "Boring topic"; Engaging reason "Interesting examples (applications)"
source: Chavan, P. & Mitra, R. (2022). Tcherly: A teacher-facing dashboard for online video lectures. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3) (Analysis of Algorithms examples).

## When it can help
In the Tcherly study, students marked as easy and/or engaging the moment a graph colouring lecture showed a practical use in newspaper printing, and the opening of an insertion sort lecture where a teaching assistant sorted answer papers by marks.

## Try it
Find one everyday situation the concept explains. Show it before the formal definition, or right after it as a "where you will see this" moment.

## How to tell if it helped
Engaging clicks with the reason "Interesting examples (applications)" should appear around the example, and Boring clicks should fall.
