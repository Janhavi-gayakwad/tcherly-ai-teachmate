# Embed short questions in the video
summary: Insert brief questions at intervals in a recorded lecture that students answer before continuing.
helps_with: boredom and mind wandering in long videos; students skipping through stretches; Boring reason "Too slow or repetitive teaching"
source: Szpunar, K. K., Khan, N. Y. & Schacter, D. L. (2013). Interpolated memory tests reduce mind wandering and improve learning of online lectures. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 110(16).

## When it can help
Szpunar and colleagues found that brief tests between segments of a video lecture reduced mind wandering, increased note-taking and improved learning.

## Try it
Add a quick question at the end of each segment of the video (a pause screen, a quiz in your LMS, or a "pause and try" prompt).

## How to tell if it helped
Boring clicks and skipping in the long stretch should fall; Engaging clicks may rise right after the questions.
