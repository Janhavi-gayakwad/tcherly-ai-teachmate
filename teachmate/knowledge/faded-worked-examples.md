# Faded worked examples
summary: Start with a fully worked example, then give examples with more and more steps left for students to complete.
helps_with: moving from watching to doing; students who follow the demonstration but cannot apply it; in-class practice after a difficult video
source: Renkl, A. & Atkinson, R. K. (2003). Structuring the transition from example study to problem solving in cognitive skill acquisition: A cognitive load perspective. Educational Psychologist, 38(1).

## When it can help
Gradually removing worked steps ("fading") helps students move from studying examples to solving problems on their own.

## Try it
Prepare a few versions of the same task: fully worked, last steps blank, most steps blank. Use them in sequence in class or as follow-up practice.

## How to tell if it helped
Students should complete the later versions with fewer errors; related video segments should draw fewer Difficult clicks next time.
