# Fix audio quality in recorded lectures
summary: Make sure students can hear clearly; poor sound turns into boredom and difficulty that have nothing to do with the content.
helps_with: free-text comments such as "voice is too low" or "cannot hear"; Boring reason "Presentation style" in a lecture capture; students skipping a stretch
source: Chavan, P. & Mitra, R. (2022). Tcherly: A teacher-facing dashboard for online video lectures. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3) (Microprocessor lecture capture example); Newman, E. J. & Schwarz, N. (2018). Good sound, good research: How audio quality influences perceptions of the research and researcher. Science Communication, 40(2).

## When it can help
In the Tcherly study, students used the "Other" option to report audio problems in a lecture capture. Newman and Schwarz found that poor audio makes listeners judge the content and the speaker less favourably, even when the content is the same.

## Try it
Use a clip-on or USB microphone, record a short test before each session, and normalise the volume when editing. For an existing video, add captions or re-record only the worst stretch.

## How to tell if it helped
Audio comments under "Other" should disappear, and skipping or Boring clicks in that stretch should fall.
