# Remove clutter and highlight what matters
summary: Cut decorative or extra material from slides and use cues (highlighting, arrows, zoom) to point to what is being explained.
helps_with: reason "Unclear or cluttered presentation"; comments like "code on screen is too small to read"
source: Mayer, R. E. (2009). Multimedia Learning (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press (coherence and signalling principles).

## When it can help
Mayer's coherence principle says people learn better when extraneous material is removed; the signalling principle says cues that highlight the essential material help.

## Try it
Use a large font (and zoom into code), show one idea per slide, and highlight the line or part being discussed.

## How to tell if it helped
"Unclear or cluttered presentation" should fall, and comments about readability should disappear.
