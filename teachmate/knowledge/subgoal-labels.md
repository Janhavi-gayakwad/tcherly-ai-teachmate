# Label the subgoals of a procedure
summary: Group the steps of a worked example under short labels that name what each group achieves.
helps_with: long demonstrations where students lose track of the purpose of steps; "Not enough explanation / examples"
source: Margulieux, L. E., Guzdial, M. & Catrambone, R. (2012). Subgoal-labeled instructional material improves performance and transfer in learning to develop mobile applications. Proceedings of the ACM International Computing Education Research conference (ICER 2012).

## When it can help
Margulieux and colleagues found that labelling the subgoals in programming instructions improved learners' performance and transfer to new problems.

## Try it
Split the demonstration into a few named stages (for example "declare the array", "fill it", "process each element") and show the label on screen as each stage starts.

## How to tell if it helped
Difficult clicks should become less concentrated at the transitions between stages.
