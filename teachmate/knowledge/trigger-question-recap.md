# Trigger question followed by a structured recap
summary: Pose a question that makes students compare or decide, then answer it with a short numbered recap of the key points.
helps_with: low engagement near the end of a lecture; students losing the thread across several ideas; wanting a clear summary
source: Chavan, P. & Mitra, R. (2022). Tcherly: A teacher-facing dashboard for online video lectures. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3) (Operating Systems and Microprocessor examples).

## When it can help
In the Tcherly study, an Operating Systems lecture ended with the question "Which is best between segmented and paged memory?" followed by a bullet-point comparison. Net engagement rose sharply in that minute and students named "Good explanation" as the main reason. A Microprocessor lecture showed the same pattern with a numbered recap.

## Try it
Before a summary, ask a question that needs the ideas just taught (compare, choose, predict). Pause briefly, then walk through the answer as a few numbered points.

## How to tell if it helped
Engaging and Easy clicks should rise in the recap minute, with "Good explanation" among the reasons.
