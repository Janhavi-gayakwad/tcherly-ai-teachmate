# Pre-teach key terms and components
summary: Before a complex explanation, briefly introduce the names and roles of its key parts or terms.
helps_with: reasons "Complex terms (jargon)" and "I'm missing basics"; difficulty right at the start of a new topic
source: Mayer, R. E., Mathias, A. & Wetzell, K. (2002). Fostering understanding of multimedia messages through pre-training: Evidence for a two-stage theory of mental model construction. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied, 8(3).

## When it can help
Mayer and colleagues found that learning the names and characteristics of key components first helped learners understand a later explanation of how the whole system works.

## Try it
Add a short glossary slide or a one-minute preview of the key terms before the difficult segment, or share it as pre-reading.

## How to tell if it helped
"Complex terms (jargon)" and "I'm missing basics" should fall in the following segment.
