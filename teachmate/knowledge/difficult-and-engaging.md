# Reading difficulty together with engagement
summary: Difficulty is not a problem by itself; what matters is whether students who found a part difficult also found it engaging.
helps_with: deciding whether a difficult segment needs changing; interpreting the Venn overlaps of Difficult with Engaging or Boring
source: Chavan, P. & Mitra, R. (2022). Tcherly: A teacher-facing dashboard for online video lectures. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3) (section on cognitive-affective dynamics).

## When it can help
The Tcherly authors read a large Difficult+Engaging overlap as productive challenge: students were stretched but still with the lecture. Difficult clicks together with Boring clicks, or engagement collapsing as difficulty rises, suggest students are losing the thread. Engagement recovering after a hard start suggests the class adjusted to the new level.

## Try it
Before changing a difficult segment, compare its Difficult+Engaging and Difficult+Boring overlaps. Keep productive challenge; add support where difficulty comes with boredom.

## How to tell if it helped
After a change, the Difficult+Boring overlap in that segment should shrink while difficulty stays reasonable.
