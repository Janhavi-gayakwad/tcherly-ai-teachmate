# Help students see why the material matters
summary: Make the usefulness of a topic explicit, or ask students to write how it connects to their lives or goals.
helps_with: Boring reasons "Not meaningful content" or "Boring topic"; low engagement on foundational material
source: Hulleman, C. S. & Harackiewicz, J. M. (2009). Promoting interest and performance in high school science classes. Science, 326(5958).

## When it can help
Hulleman and Harackiewicz found that asking students to write about the relevance of course material increased their interest and performance, especially for students who did not expect to do well.

## Try it
Open the topic with where it is used, or end with a short writing prompt: "Where could you use this?"

## How to tell if it helped
"Not meaningful content" and "Boring topic" reasons should fall in that segment.
