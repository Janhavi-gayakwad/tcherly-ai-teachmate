# Keep videos short and personal
summary: Prefer several short videos to one long recording, and keep a personal, conversational style.
helps_with: engagement that declines over a long video; Boring clicks late in a lecture; students skipping ahead
source: Guo, P. J., Kim, J. & Rubin, R. (2014). How video production affects student engagement: An empirical study of MOOC videos. Proceedings of the First ACM Conference on Learning @ Scale (L@S 2014).

## When it can help
In a large study of MOOC videos, Guo and colleagues found that shorter videos were much more engaging, and that informal, personal styles (such as the instructor's face or hand-drawn tablet explanations) were more engaging than slides alone.

## Try it
Split long recordings into short videos of one idea each; show your face or draw by hand at key points instead of only reading slides.

## How to tell if it helped
Engagement should hold up better towards the end, with fewer late Boring clicks and less skipping.
