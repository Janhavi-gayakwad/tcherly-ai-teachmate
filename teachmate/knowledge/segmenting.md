# Break long explanations into short segments
summary: Split a long, continuous explanation into short parts with a natural pause point between them.
helps_with: reason "Too fast"; long stretches where difficulty builds up; students jumping back to re-watch
source: Mayer, R. E. (2009). Multimedia Learning (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press (segmenting principle); Mayer, R. E. & Chandler, P. (2001). When learning is just a click away: Does simple user interaction foster deeper understanding of multimedia messages? Journal of Educational Psychology, 93(2).

## When it can help
Mayer and Chandler found that letting learners control the pace of an explanation in segments improved understanding compared with one continuous presentation.

## Try it
Cut the video at the end of each idea (or add chapter markers) and add a one-line recap or question at each break.

## How to tell if it helped
"Too fast" reasons and re-watching in that stretch should fall.
