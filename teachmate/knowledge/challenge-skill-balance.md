# Match the challenge to students' level
summary: Boredom can come from material that is too easy or too hard; the reasons tell which, and the fixes differ.
helps_with: Boring reasons "Too easy for me" versus "Too difficult for me"; a class split between Easy and Difficult
source: Csikszentmihalyi, M. (1990). Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience. Harper & Row; Pekrun, R. (2006). The control-value theory of achievement emotions. Educational Psychology Review, 18(4).

## When it can help
Flow theory and control-value theory both link boredom to a mismatch between the challenge and the learner's skill. "Too easy for me" calls for stretch or skipping options; "Too difficult for me" calls for support.

## Try it
For "too easy": add chapter markers so students can skip what they know, and an optional harder extension. For "too difficult": add a worked example or pre-teach the basics.

## How to tell if it helped
Boring clicks should fall in the segment, and the split between Easy and Difficult should narrow.
