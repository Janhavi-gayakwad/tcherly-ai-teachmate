# Just-in-time teaching
summary: Use students' responses to pre-class material to plan the next live class around their difficulties.
helps_with: flipped or blended courses; deciding what to revisit in class after students watch a video
source: Novak, G. M., Patterson, E. T., Gavrin, A. D. & Christian, W. (1999). Just-in-Time Teaching: Blending Active Learning with Web Technology. Prentice Hall.

## When it can help
Tcherly feedback on a video watched before class works like just-in-time warm-up responses: it shows which segments to spend class time on.

## Try it
Before class, check the most difficult and most boring segments and the reasons students gave; build the first part of the class around them, using students' own words.

## How to tell if it helped
Students should recognise their difficulties being addressed; in the next video, related segments should draw fewer Difficult clicks.
