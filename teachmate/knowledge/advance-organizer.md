# Give a roadmap at the start (advance organizer)
summary: Begin with a short overview of what is coming and how it connects to what students already know.
helps_with: confusion at transitions between topics; reason "I'm missing basics"; students skipping around in a long video
source: Ausubel, D. P. (1960). The use of advance organizers in the learning and retention of meaningful verbal material. Journal of Educational Psychology, 51(5).

## When it can help
Ausubel proposed that an introductory "organizer" linking new material to existing knowledge helps learners structure and retain it.

## Try it
Open with a one-slide map of the lecture's parts and how they connect to the previous class; return to it at each transition.

## How to tell if it helped
Difficult clicks should become less concentrated at topic transitions, and "I'm missing basics" should fall.
