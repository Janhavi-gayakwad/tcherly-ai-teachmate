# Explain with a schematic built up step by step
summary: Show a structure or process as a diagram that appears piece by piece while you narrate it.
helps_with: abstract concepts students find difficult; "Unclear or cluttered presentation"; "Not enough explanation / examples"
source: Chavan, P. & Mitra, R. (2022). Tcherly: A teacher-facing dashboard for online video lectures. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3) (Operating Systems example).

## When it can help
In the Tcherly study, the opening of a lecture that explained segmented memory with a schematic and animation drew Easy and Engaging feedback, with "Good explanation" and "Presentation style" as the main reasons.

## Try it
Replace a dense slide with a diagram that builds up in steps (animation or drawing live). Narrate each step as it appears instead of showing everything at once.

## How to tell if it helped
In that segment, Difficult reasons such as "Unclear or cluttered presentation" should fall and Easy or Engaging clicks should rise.
